Patterns in the UFO files

Every page that reads like a sighting report is scanned for concrete, recurring details: glows and halos, humming or total silence, smells, hovering, right-angle turns, engines stalling, marks left on the ground. Dates and places on those pages show when reports bunched into waves. Records that describe similar things are linked across agencies and decades. Everything below updates when new files are released.
