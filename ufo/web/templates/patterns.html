Patterns in the UFO files

Every page that reads like a sighting report is scanned for concrete, recurring details: glows and halos, humming or total silence, smells, hovering, right-angle turns, engines stalling, marks left on the ground. Dates and places on those pages show when reports bunched into waves. Sighting accounts are tagged with what was observed and grouped into recurring sighting types; records whose accounts describe the same kind of event are linked across agencies and decades. Everything below updates when new files are released.

Markers in the text, the FOIA exemption codes stamped where text was removed and the blacked-out blocks that OCR reads as block characters, appear in {{ rd.overview.marked | n }} of the {{ rd.overview.with_text | n }} records with text, {{ rd.overview.heavy | n }} of them heavily (at least 2 markers per page). {{ rd.overview.flagged_without_markers | n }} flagged records show no marker in their text (the redaction is a blank box the OCR cannot see), and {{ rd.overview.marked_without_flag | n }} carry markers without being flagged: the historical files. A blank scanned page could be a redaction or an empty page, so blank pages are not counted.
