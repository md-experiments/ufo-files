Patterns in the UFO files

Every page that reads like a sighting report is scanned for concrete, recurring details: glows and halos, humming or total silence, smells, hovering, right-angle turns, engines stalling, marks left on the ground. Dates and places on those pages show when reports bunched into waves. Sighting accounts are tagged with what was observed and grouped into recurring sighting types; records whose accounts describe the same kind of event are linked across agencies and decades. Everything below updates when new files are released.

Sighting types map of {{ p.accounts | n }} sighting accounts

Each sighting page is cut into accounts (paragraphs, or one report form), and each account is tagged with what was observed: shape, colour, light, sound, movement, visible structure, effects on people, machines or the ground. A sighting type is a combination of details that recurs across records far more often than chance would give. Agency, archive series, place, decade and wording play no part. Every dot is one account, placed near accounts that describe similar events. Numbered circles mark types. Hover or tap a card to highlight its accounts, or click a dot to read it.
