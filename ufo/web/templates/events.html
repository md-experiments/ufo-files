What happens in the files

Every event, in a sentence or two

Each record is cut into episodes: one for every distinct event it describes, so a case-file section that lists a dozen sightings gets a dozen summaries. Every episode is classed by what happened (seen in the sky, picked up by instruments, a close encounter, physical evidence, observed from space, or no specific event) and by the details it reports, which sort it into a subcategory: over water, seen from an aircraft, engines failing, landed, occupants, markings on the ground. The records in the bundled snapshot were read and classed one by one, not matched by keyword.

How the events sort class, then subcategory

The class says what kind of thing happened. The subcategory is derived from the details each episode reports, in a fixed order of priority (an episode that reports both a radar track and a landing counts under close encounters, because effects and proximity outrank how it was seen).
